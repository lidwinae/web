Winaeth

A story-led dimension shaped by flowers, memory, and corruption.

Overview Journey Places Characters Endings Development

Overview

One island, a fallen duchess, and a flower that should never have landed.

Winaeth is a Minecraft dimension built around a guided story. The player follows a route through forests, villages, valleys, and a corrupted castle while learning what happened to the people who once lived there.

Winaeth was once peaceful. Its people worked as farmers, miners, florists, lumberjacks, tailors, and weapon makers. Castle Velrathia and Hummervale Village were connected by Asterfall Bridge.

Everything changed when a meteor struck near the castle. The bridge was destroyed, unfamiliar Starwinae Blooms appeared, and a dark haze covered the castle and the surrounding forest. Communication with Hummervale stopped.

Entering Winaeth

The Great Tree is grown, not built.

To reach Winaeth, the player first creates a Starwinae Bloom and plants it above a crying obsidian block. When the bloom grows, it becomes the Winaeth Great Tree.

A narrow doorway inside the trunk contains the inactive Winaeth Anchor. Using a second Starwinae Bloom on the lower anchor consumes the bloom, activates both anchor blocks, and opens a safe portal inside the tree.

  1. 1Plant a Starwinae Bloom above crying obsidian.
  2. 2Let the bloom grow into the Winaeth Great Tree.
  3. 3Use another bloom to activate the Winaeth Anchor.
  4. 4Enter the portal and arrive in Lydrosilva.

The journey

From Lydrosilva to Castle Velrathia.

01

Lydrosilva

A large green forest and the player's arrival point. Camps along its paths belong to a wandering trader who became lost while traveling. He offers to guide the player to Hummervale.

02

The Lydrosilva Warden

The forest's natural protector sees unknown travelers as a possible threat. It is not corrupted. The player only learns its real purpose after the encounter.

03

Hummervale

A village protected by the flowers growing around it. Generations of using peony and sunflower in food, medicine, and daily life have given its people a natural resistance to Starwinae corruption.

04

Florenmere Valley

A flower-filled valley forming a natural barrier between Hummervale and the corrupted land. Floren of the Hollow Reed lives here in hiding.

05

The Starwinae Wound

The crater created by the meteor. Its guardian protects the material needed to create Starwinae Essence and the Starwinae Dagger.

06

Astersilva and Castle Velrathia

Astersilva surrounds the castle as a contained ring of corruption. Beyond it, the player finds the Duchess and eventually Vyrwinae, the true source of the disaster.

Characters

The story is divided between people who know different parts of the truth.

Wandering Trader

A lost traveler who becomes the player's first guide. His llamas remain at one of the camps, and he is much more capable in combat than he first appears.

Hummond

Hummervale's village elder and master gunsmith. He helps the player understand the past and records the world events reported by different players.

Floren of the Hollow Reed

A former castle servant who escaped while partially corrupted. Peony keeps his condition dormant, and living with it taught him how Starwinae Essence behaves.

Duchess Velrathia

Winaeth's former ruler. She ordered the strange blooms to be studied and was eventually overtaken by their corruption. By the time the player arrives, she can no longer be saved.

Vyrwinae

A dragon born from the egg carried by the meteor. Its power spread through the meteor, the blooms, the forest, and Castle Velrathia.

Royal Alchemist

A researcher called by the Duchess during the early stages of her corruption. His journals preserve the difference between stopping corruption and curing it.

Flowers and corruption

Three flowers shape the outcome.

Starwinae Bloom

Spreads the meteor's influence into living things and the land. Its power can also be stabilized and used in weapons.

Peony

Makes the corruption dormant and prevents it from getting worse, but cannot remove corruption already inside the body.

Sunflower

Can cure Starwinae corruption before it reaches its final stage. Discovering this changes what the player can save.

Endings

Defeating Vyrwinae ends the danger, but not every version of the story.

Neutral Ending

The player returns to Hummervale alone and tells Hummond what happened at the castle. The village finally learns the truth, but Floren remains hidden in Florenmere Valley.

Show Secret Happy Ending spoilers

Secret Happy Ending

Using a sunflower-powered weapon for the Duchess's final hit and later defeating Vyrwinae reveals the Forgotten Greenhouse. The Royal Alchemist's journals lead to the Sunflower Remedy.

The remedy cannot save the Duchess, but Floren is only partially corrupted. The player cures him and accompanies him back to Hummervale, where Hummond welcomes him home.

Development status

Winaeth is still being built.

The current Fabric 26.2 prototype contains early work for the Great Tree gateway, portal blocks, Starwinae Bloom, story trader and llama entities, navigation targets, objective-compass assets, items, and sounds.

The locations, characters, bosses, quests, dialogue, and endings described above are the intended project direction. Details may change before a public release.
